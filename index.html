We've all been there

A guilty pleasure at 2am. A thumbnail too weird not to click. A clickbait title that turned out to be nothing. You watch for thirty seconds and move on.

YouTube doesn't move on. Your home page fills up with more of the same for weeks.

YT Regret lets you take those views back. Once a video is out of your watch history, it stops steering your recommendations.

Regret anything, anywhere

A button where you need it, and rules for the things you'd rather not think about.

Videos

One click on any video

A Regret button sits next to Like and Share on every video. Click it and the video is out of your watch history on the spot, before it can shape what YouTube shows you next.

Watch history

Clean up past mistakes

Every entry in your watch history gets its own Regret button. One click and it's gone, without digging through menus.

Shorts

Doomscroll without consequences

Regret a single Short, or switch on Auto and every Short is removed from your history after you swipe past it.

Channels

Always regret a channel

Click Always regret on a channel's page, and its videos and Shorts never stay in your history, however long you watch.

Options

Your rules, and a log of what happened

Turn features on and off, manage your channel list, see what's waiting to be removed, and check which rule removed each video.

Clickbait filter

Remove anything you leave within N seconds of playback. Ads don't count.

Never while you watch

Automatic removals happen a few seconds after you leave a video, so the view can't sneak back in.

It keeps trying

If YouTube hasn't recorded a view yet, the removal is queued and retried in the background.

Private by design

YT Regret uses the same "Remove from watch history" action as YouTube's own history page, in your browser, signed in as you.

No servers

Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. The only requests go to youtube.com, for your own account.

No tracking

No analytics, no accounts, no ads. Your settings stay in your browser and sync through Chrome if you use it.

Questions

Does this really change my recommendations?

Your watch history is one of the main signals YouTube uses for recommendations. Removing a video from it is the same as removing it yourself on the history page, so it stops counting towards what YouTube shows you next.

Why not use the YouTube API?

YouTube's public API hasn't offered access to watch history since 2016. YT Regret works from inside youtube.com instead, doing what the history page does when you click ✕.

Which browsers does it work in?

It's built for desktop Chrome and installed from the Chrome Web Store. It doesn't work in YouTube's mobile apps.

Does it need to stay open?

Removals run while a YouTube tab is open. Anything still queued when you close YouTube resumes the next time you open it.

A button disappeared. What happened?

YouTube changes its page layout from time to time. If a button stops showing up, please open an issue. After the extension updates, reload your open YouTube tabs once as well.
