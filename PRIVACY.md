# Privacy policy

YT Regret does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data.

- The extension only runs on www.youtube.com, in your browser.
- To remove a video from your watch history, it sends YouTube the same request YouTube's own history page sends, signed in as you. It makes no other requests, except when you add a channel on the options page: it then loads that channel's YouTube page to read its name and ID.
- Your settings and channel list are saved in Chrome's extension storage, and synced by Chrome if you use Chrome sync. The removal queue and recent activity (video IDs and titles) are kept in your browser's storage for youtube.com.
- There are no servers, analytics or tracking. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

Questions: https://github.com/spite/yt-regret/issues
